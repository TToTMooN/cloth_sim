Interactive Cloth Simulation

Connect to the live simulation. Only one participant can control the scene at a time, with a 30 second limit.

Not connected

When you are the active controller, use the mouse or keyboard to send inputs.

How it works

  • When you connect, you either get control immediately or join a queue.
  • Each controller session lasts 30 seconds, then the next person takes over.
  • Control inputs are sent over a WebSocket channel to the simulation server.
